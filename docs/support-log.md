# Support contact log

One line per contact. Newest at the bottom. Not a tool — a list.

Format: `date  who  what happened  → what you did`

**Why bother:** after roughly 20 lines the same three problems will be staring at you. Those three
are your next engineering work, and they are better information than any feature you could guess
at. This file is also where the FAQ comes from, and an FAQ is what stops you answering the same
email forever.

Triage rules, reply templates and how to read a diagnostic block: [support.md](support.md).
